Signing in with single sign-on (Authelia)

Both Nextcloud and Forgejo can be accessed with a single shared login provided by Authelia. Your account has already been created — there is no self-service signup, so if you cannot log in, ask the administrator to add you.

To sign in:

  • Forgejo – on the login page, click Sign in with authelia. The first time, you may be asked to enter your existing Forgejo password once to link your account to Authelia; after that, the Authelia button is all you need.
  • Nextcloud – on the login page, click Log in with Authelia. Your existing files and shares are kept — the single sign-on simply logs you into the same account.

You will be sent to the Authelia portal to enter your username and password, then returned to the app. Once you have signed in to Authelia, moving between the two apps does not require typing your password again.

Forgot your password? Use the Reset password link on the Authelia portal.
